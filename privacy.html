Privacy policy

How Kakobuyspreadsheet handles data. This covers our site only — Kakobuy.com has its own policy.

A free, static Kakobuy tool hub (spreadsheet, QC Finder, converter, coupons, FAQ). You do not create an account here. We do not take card payments on this domain.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-J7XGTCP578) to see which pages are opened and which Kakobuy buy links are clicked. Google may set cookies and process usage data under its own terms. We do not sell advertising audiences from that data.

Affiliate links

Product and signup links go to Kakobuy with an affiliate code. Kakobuy then processes your order and account. Their privacy terms apply on kakobuy.com.

Local storage

Some UI preferences (for example dismissing the install banner, or remembering that you added the app to your home screen) may be stored in your browser so we do not repeat them every visit. That data stays on your device.

Progressive Web App

You can install Kakobuyspreadsheet as an app on your phone or computer. A service worker may cache core pages and icons for faster reopening. Product catalog images are not fully cached offline.

Third-party services

Discord, Google Sheets / Forms, and Kakobuy are separate services. If you open them from this site, their own policies apply.

Your rights

Browsing here does not require a customer database.
