FAQ — Kakobuy QC Finder

What is Kakobuy QC Finder?

A free tool that shows warehouse quality-check photos after you paste a product link. Community names: find QC, QC find, QCFinder.

Is it free / do I need an account?

100% free, no Kakobuy login required to browse historical QC for a link.

Which platforms work?

Taobao, Weidian, 1688, Kakobuy, and common agent URLs. The converter extracts the marketplace ID.

No photos found?

Nobody ordered that link yet via the agent. Try the raw Weidian/Taobao URL or pick a find from the spreadsheet that already has QC.

Are these the exact pair I’ll get?

No — they’re earlier orders of the same link. They show batch expectation; your own QC still arrives after you purchase.
