Who runs Kakobuyspreadsheet Finder

An independent Kakobuy Item Finder, spreadsheet and QC Finder — free tools for haul planning, not a storefront.

Kakobuyspreadsheet Finder is an independently maintained hub of 4,000+ curated Kakobuy finds with warehouse-style QC photos, a link converter, shipping estimate, and coupons. It began as a private haul notebook and opened publicly so buyers could skip dead links and stock photos.

Who writes the pages

Pages are written by the Kakobuyspreadsheet editorial team — people who order through shopping agents, wait for warehouse QC, and ship internationally. We are not Kakobuy staff, we do not run a warehouse, and we do not manufacture the goods listed here.

How a find gets listed

A card is added when there is a live marketplace / Kakobuy-ready link and at least one usable product or QC-style photo. Popular finds are re-checked. Dead seller links are removed or replaced rather than left broken.

What we will not claim

We do not promise customs clearance. We do not call replicas “authentic”. We do not hide affiliate relationships: if you register or order through a Kakobuy link on this site, we may earn a commission. Browsing stays free.
